My tech watch method

  1. 01

    Collect

    I follow YouTube channels and LinkedIn accounts of developers and tool vendors: new releases, real-world feedback and security alerts.

  2. 02

    Sort

    I share useful links on my Discord server. My NEXUS bot, written in Python, automatically files them in the #veille-informatique channel.

  3. 03

    Check and summarize

    Before keeping a piece of information, I trace it back to the official source (vendor announcement, documentation, OWASP, CERT-FR). I then write a dated summary sheet, linked to my projects.

Cybersecurity & ransomware

Ransomware encrypts an organization's data and demands a ransom. Hospitals, local authorities and small businesses are frequent targets. The European NIS2 directive requires many more companies to protect themselves and report their incidents.

The end of passwords

Passkeys replace the password with a cryptographic key stored on the device and unlocked by fingerprint, face or PIN. There is no secret to type anymore, so nothing left to steal through phishing.
